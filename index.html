House Points + PCQP

Everything in this section is connected. Paid customer sales drive the annual sales estimate, House Points and conservative GP. PCQP is then calculated from conservative GP. The same annualized spend is used for the renewal check and renewal discount.

1. House Points Formula

Example: ₱500 paid spend = 5 House Points. The system floors partial blocks.

2. PCQP Formula

PCQP is based directly on Gross Profit, not on a separate spend block.
PCQP = FLOOR(Conservative GP × 10%). Conservative GP = Paid Sales × 12.96%.

Renewal Package Basis

This selection determines which first-year Base Privilege Cost is carried into the renewal calculation. It is independent of the Target Sales Source.

5. Renewal Rules — Paid Renewal

Renewal is not free. Every member may renew by paying the renewal fee. The annual sales / HP / PCQP requirements determine whether they qualify for a renewal discount. If the requirements are not met, the member can still renew but pays the full renewal fee. Higher PCQP = higher accumulated conservative GP / paid spending = higher potential renewal discount.
Automatic renewal thresholds. Minimum House Points and Minimum PCQP are not manually entered. They are automatically calculated from the Minimum Annual Paid Sales using the current House Points and PCQP formulas. The minimum annual sales is the only spending threshold you edit.

Renewal Discount Tiers

Only the PCQP threshold and discount percentage are editable. The system automatically selects the highest tier reached by the customer's annualized PCQP. The renewal fee can never become negative. A member may always renew by paying the base fee; the tier only determines the discount.
Renewal economics: the renewal uses the same base privilege package as the selected first-year proposal. The system carries that same Base Privilege Cost into Year 2, compares it with the Base Renewal Fee, and shows the renewal gap/surplus. The member can always renew by paying the fee. The Minimum Annual Paid Sales is the discount gateway, while PCQP determines the discount tier.
